# Sponsor rewards — how to claim them

- **Supporter ($5/mo):** your name lands in the README thank-you list with the next release.
- **Creator ($15/mo):** vote on the next check — comment your vote on the roadmap issue (#1).
- **Studio ($50/mo) — monthly video QC review:** open an issue titled `Sponsor QC review` and write only "ready" (no video link in public). We reply in that issue with private hand-off instructions, run the full Faceless QC on your video, and post the verdict summary back in the issue.
- **Feature sponsor (one-time $100):** open an issue titled `Feature sponsor` naming the shipped check you want your name tied to.

Rewards are honoured in sponsor order, within 5 working days of the sponsorship landing.
